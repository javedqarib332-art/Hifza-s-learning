Clarity Corner is for everyday stress and learning. It is not medical advice or a replacement for professional support.

Take a moment

You are okay. Let us slow things down together.

  1. Put both feet flat on the floor.
  2. Breathe out slowly, three times, longer than you breathe in.
  3. Name three things you can see around you.

If things feel too big or you feel unsafe, please talk to someone you trust or a local support service right away.
